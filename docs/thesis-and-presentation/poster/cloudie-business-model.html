Why now in Malaysia

1PDPA (Amendment) Act 2024: data breach notification and data protection officers are now required.
2Cyber Security Act 2024 (Act 854): new duties for national critical information infrastructure (NCII) sectors.
3Files are the way in: invoices, CVs and forms arrive as attachments every day.
